SET 18 "ENCHANTED WILDS" NOTES (patch 18.3, Unreal Engine client)

Round structure: stage 1 = 1-1 carousel, 1-2..1-4 PvE. From stage 2: x-1..x-3 PvP, x-4 carousel (back this set),
x-5..x-6 PvP, x-7 PvE (2 Krugs, 3 Wolves, 4 Raptors, 5 Drake, 6+ Herald). Augments at 2-1, 3-2, 4-2 (silver/gold/prismatic).
Opening encounters can change rules (e.g. levels cost 2 XP less, free rerolls each stage, all gold or prismatic augments).

Wisps (set mechanic): every other shop, the rightmost shop slot holds a Wisp (a consumable costing 0 to 45 gold) instead of
a visible champion. You can buy 1 Wisp per round (2 with Blossom 9), only in planning. Buying reveals the champion under it;
an unbought Wisp fades at end of planning and the champion becomes buyable. Categories: Combat, Gold/XP, Shop, Item, Champion,
Risky, Misc. After stage 5 every other Wisp is a Combat Wisp. Judge a Wisp like a small augment: cheap econ/XP Wisps early,
combat Wisps when stabilizing. Blossom 3 upgrades Wisps, Blossom 5 puts a Wisp in every shop, Blossom 7 gives 4 gold per Wisp.

Units and board quirks:
- Lux (5 cost, Avatar) has 9 trait forms (Blackthorn, Blossom, Coven, Elderwood, Fae, Inferno, Lunar, Primal, Solar); her chosen
  trait counts twice. All forms share one pool of 9 copies.
- Adaptor units (Akali, Gromp, Kog'Maw, Master Yi, Nidalee) have AD/AP forms that share one pool; Gnar small/big share one pool.
- Elder Dragon (5 cost) takes 2 team slots. Riftbeast 10 gives +2 team size.
- Elderwood places plants (Stonebark Tree, Lifebloom, Deepwood Protector) on hexes: they are not champions.
- Riftbeast 5+: every 3 combats the next shop is overrun with Riftbeasts. Inferno 3/5/7: 1/2/4 shop slots roll one cost higher.
- Coven gains Essence on kills and losses and cashes out at thresholds (40/85/130/185/250/365/500...). Rival: Kha'Zix and Rengar,
  normally only one can be fielded.
- Solar: 3/5/8 unique 3-star units; at 8 a 3-star ascends to 4-star every few seconds. 4-star units exist.
- Items: 10 components (Frying Pan included). Spatula emblems: Fae, Elderwood, Blackthorn, Blossom, Sprykin, Inferno, Primal,
  Lunar. Frying Pan emblems: Hunter, Vanguard, Brawler, Spellweaver, Ravager, Rapidfire, Executioner, Invoker.
  Pan + Pan = Tactician's Shield, Spatula + Pan = Tactician's Cape, Spatula + Spatula = Tactician's Crown (+1 team size).

Meta direction on 18.3 (TFTAcademy): Juggernaut boards (Ashe, Sivir, Amumu) and Aphelios lines are strong, AD carries like
fast 9, AP has level 8 options (Ahri, Zyra, Soraka). 3-cost rerolls: Kha'Zix Lunarwood, Azir Rammus. See the comp library.
